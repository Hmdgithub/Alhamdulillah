What the Qur’an teaches about marriage

Marriage is described not merely as companionship, but as a relationship marked by tranquility, affection, mercy, protection, and mutual care.

Surah Ar-Rum • 30:21
وَمِنْ آيَاتِهِ أَنْ خَلَقَ لَكُم مِّنْ أَنفُسِكُمْ أَزْوَاجًا لِّتَسْكُنُوا إِلَيْهَا وَجَعَلَ بَيْنَكُم مَّوَدَّةً وَرَحْمَةً

“And among His signs is that He created for you spouses from among yourselves so that you may find tranquility in them; and He placed between you affection and mercy.”

A reminder that a strong marriage grows through sakinah, mawaddah, and rahmah.
Surah Al-Baqarah • 2:187
هُنَّ لِبَاسٌ لَّكُمْ وَأَنتُمْ لِبَاسٌ لَّهُنَّ

“They are clothing for you and you are clothing for them.”

Spouses can be a source of comfort, dignity, protection, closeness, and reassurance for one another.
Surah Al-Furqan • 25:74
رَبَّنَا هَبْ لَنَا مِنْ أَزْوَاجِنَا وَذُرِّيَّاتِنَا قُرَّةَ أَعْيُنٍ وَاجْعَلْنَا لِلْمُتَّقِينَ إِمَامًا

“Our Lord, grant us from our spouses and offspring comfort to our eyes and make us an example for the righteous.”

A dua for a home that brings peace, joy, righteous growth, and lasting goodness.
Building a beautiful home

Lessons for a stronger marriage

The first three months are only the beginning. Every day offers another chance to choose kindness, understanding, patience, and gratitude.

01

Listen before you answer

Try to understand the feeling behind the words. Being heard can turn tension into closeness.

02

Protect each other’s dignity

Correct privately, appreciate publicly, and never make disagreement an excuse for disrespect.

03

Make mercy a habit

Forgive small mistakes, give one another room to grow, and remember that neither partner is perfect.

04

Share the weight

Celebrate responsibilities as a team. Support is showing up when life gets heavy.

05

Keep gratitude visible

Say “Alhamdulillah” for the little things, and “JazakAllahu khairan” for the things that matter.

06

Grow together spiritually

Make salah, dua, Qur’an, honesty, and good character part of the shared direction of your home.
